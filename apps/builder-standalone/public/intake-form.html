Step 7 of 8

Features & compliance

Choose the features and protections you want on your site. Everything marked "Included" comes standard at no extra charge.

SEO Optimization Included

Search Engine Optimization helps your site appear in Google results. We optimize page titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt text, sitemap, and robots.txt so customers can find you online.

ADA Accessibility Included

Makes your site usable by people with disabilities — screen reader support, keyboard navigation, color contrast, alt text. This is a legal requirement under the Americans with Disabilities Act and protects you from potential lawsuits.

Security Headers Included

HTTPS, content security policies, and security headers that protect your site and visitors from common web attacks. Standard best practice for any professional website.

Analytics Dashboard Recommended

See how many people visit your site, which pages they view, and where they come from. We use privacy-friendly analytics (no cookie consent banners needed) so your visitors aren't bothered by popups.

Privacy Policy Page Recommended

A legally-oriented privacy policy page explaining what data your site collects and how it's used. Note: This is a template — we recommend having it reviewed by a legal professional for your specific business.

Email Signup / Newsletter

Capture email addresses from visitors who want to stay in touch. Integrates with Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar services.

Live Chat Widget Add-on

Let visitors chat with you in real time. Great for capturing leads who might not fill out a form. Integrates with Tawk.to (free) or other providers.

Ongoing Maintenance Plan Add-on

Monthly retainer ($50-150/mo) for weekly backups, uptime monitoring, content updates, and quarterly compliance checks. Peace of mind that your site stays healthy.

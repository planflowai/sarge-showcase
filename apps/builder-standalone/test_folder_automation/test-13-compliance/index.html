Why Accessibility Matters

Over 1 billion people worldwide live with disabilities. An accessible web isn't just a legal requirement—it's a moral imperative and a business advantage. We help you create experiences that include everyone.

Our Commitment

We follow WCAG guidelines, use semantic HTML, ensure keyboard navigability, provide sufficient color contrast, and add appropriate ARIA attributes to make the web work for all.
